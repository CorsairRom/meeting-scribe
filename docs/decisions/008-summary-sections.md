# ADR-008: The summary has a Decisions section

## Status

`active` - approved by the user on 2026-10-11 (F5 design review, product question OQ-1). It extends the output format
of [ADR-006](006-summary-provider-openai-compatible.md) and does not supersede it.

## Context

ADR-006 defines the reference structure of the summary: Topics, Action items and Open questions. The PRD user story
for F5 promises "topics, decisions, action items and open questions". The two disagree: a decision such as "the
release moves to Friday" has no heading of its own in the ADR format, so it would be buried as a point under a topic.
Decisions are the part of minutes people look for later, so the format must expose them.

## Options Considered

### Option A: Keep the ADR-006 format

**Description**: decisions appear as points inside topics.

**Pros**:
- No change to an active ADR; shorter summaries.

**Cons**:
- Does not match the PRD story; decisions are hard to scan for.

**Cost**: none.

### Option B: Add a `Decisions` section between Topics and Action items

**Description**: the model writes a flat list of decisions; the section is always present ("None" when empty).

**Pros**:
- Matches the PRD; one scannable list; the structure stays fixed for every meeting.

**Cons**:
- A decision may be repeated under its topic; the prompt must say to list it once, in this section.

**Cost**: one more section in the prompt, the renderer table and the tests.

---

## Decision

**We choose Option B.**

The PRD names decisions explicitly and they are what the reader scans for, so they get their own heading. ADR-006 is
not edited; this ADR records the extension and the spec (`docs/specs/summaries.md`, section 11) holds the full
format.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- The summary matches the PRD story and the structure is identical across meetings.

### Negative

- Slightly longer summaries and a prompt that must avoid duplicating a decision under a topic.

### Accepted trade-offs

- Some overlap between a topic point and a decision is tolerated rather than forcing the model to deduplicate.

### Trigger to revisit

- Real summaries show the section is usually empty or mostly duplicates topic points.

---

## Implementation notes

- The structure is `# title`, Participants, Generated by, the recognition-error note, then `## Topics`, `## Decisions`,
  `## Action items`, `## Open questions`. Headings follow the summary language.
- Affected code: the summaries prompt builder, heading table and renderer (F5.1).
- Related docs: `docs/specs/summaries.md`.
